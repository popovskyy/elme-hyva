FE-111 Add to Cart: what happens after the server says yes

Click Add to Cart on any tile. All three use the same quiet spinner, the same "Added" swap (the old label leaves before the new one comes in) and the same thumbnail that flies to the header cart. Only the moment of success is different.

Now (for comparison)

Ring and seven dots inside the dark button, over the check and the label.

1. Halo

The check draws itself, and the button's outline grows out of its edges in olive and sage, like a ripple on water. Calm, no particles.

2. Olive fill

The button fills olive from left to right with a check that draws itself, holds, then fades back to charcoal. The colour alone says "done".

3. Sparks

The heart's burst, moved out of the button: short sparks shoot from its edges onto the white card, and the check springs like the heart does.
