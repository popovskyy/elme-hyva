Elme homepage: is it too busy?

Short answer: every block is fine on its own, but the page has too many loud moments in a row. Below: the page as it is now with the problems marked, then two calmer directions with the same photos and copy. Reply with a number, or a mix.

"Now" is rebuilt from the theme code and the options picked earlier (Cinema hero, Statement run, quote + dark contact band), not a screenshot of elme-hyva.local; spacing and some details differ from the live page. Product cards in 2 are grey placeholders.

Now

Sage bar, dark hero, carousel, big statement, dark outlet band with ticker, catalogues, collage, big quote, dark contact band, Instagram, dark footer.

  1. AStripes. Four charcoal blocks (hero, Outlet, Need assistance, footer) plus the sage bar. The page reads as light / dark / light / dark, so no block stands out.
  2. BFour headlines at display size. Hero heading, the welcome H1, "Up to 80% off" and the Our Difference quote all compete for first place.
  3. CMotion everywhere. Rising hero lines, a ticker, every section fading in on scroll, animated contact icons, button sheen and ripple. Nice one at a time, busy together.
  4. DSame message three times. "Become a Stockist" is in the top bar, under the welcome text and as its own section; Our Difference repeats the welcome in long form.
  5. ENothing for a buyer who is already a stockist. No products, no Quick Order, no search for SKUs; a logged-in retailer scrolls past marketing to reach the menu.
  6. FUneven rhythm. Full-bleed bands and contained blocks alternate with different gaps, and the categories are a carousel that hides two of the eight tiles.
