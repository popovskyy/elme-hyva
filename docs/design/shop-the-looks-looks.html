FE-77 Shop the Looks: looks

Same three looks and seven pins as /shop-the-looks today, same order in every frame. All three share the new pin (dark core, white ring, shadow, soft pulse, number inside), photos at their own aspect ratio on a tint of the photo while they load, room filter with counts and a cross-fade, popover next to the pin on desktop and a bottom sheet with a drag handle on the phone.

FE53 Ceramic Vase had no Add to Cart because it is a configurable product (Sage / Sand); here it asks for the colour first, and Add this look skips it and says so. Pins, rows and chips are live: hover, click, try the filter.

1. Editorial: photo and product rail

Photo takes two thirds and alternates sides look by look; the numbered product list sits next to it and stays in view, so a row and its pin light up together.

2. Immersive: full-width scenes and a Shop this look drawer

Each look is a full-width band in the photo's own colour with the title card on it; Shop this look opens one list of every item with qty, Add to Cart and Add all.

3. Mosaic: grid of looks that opens a look sheet

The page is a grid of looks at their own shapes (wide photos span the row); a look opens in a sheet with the photo, the pins and the full product list.
