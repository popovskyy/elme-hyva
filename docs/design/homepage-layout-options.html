Elme homepage: three directions

The blocks are fine one by one; the page as a whole is not. Three whole-page directions with the same content and photos. Reply with a number, or mix, for example "1, but the categories from 2".

  • Seven headings of almost the same size, so nothing leads.
  • "Open a trade account with your ABN" is said three times (hero, welcome, Become a Stockist).
  • Text blocks and half-photo rows take turns down the page; after the hero there are few photos.
  • Nothing for a buyer who already trades with Elme: no Quick Order, catalogues sit low.

Scroll motion is the same in all three: each block rises a little and fades in once, tiles follow one after another, big photos settle into their frame. Visitors who turn animations off get a plain fade.

1. Store front

Photo-led: the hero text sits on the dark side of the photo, all eight categories show at once, Outlet and Catalogues become two equal cards, Become a Stockist is one sage band.
