UX-15 Neutral ramp options

Today the page is warm (#F5F4EF) while panels and muted text lean cool (#F8F8F8, #ECEFF1, #676F78). Pick one ramp so grounds, lines and muted text share one temperature. Both options keep ink #3A3935 and page #F5F4EF, four grounds, two line colours, and muted/subtle text at least 4.5:1 on page and surface.

Answer with 1 or 2. Phone frames are 390 wide; wide frames are 1280. Each frame shows header, product cards, a form and cart in the same ramp.

1. Warm only

Grounds, soft lines and muted/subtle text all sit on the warm side of ink and page. Cool greys leave the ramp. Muted #605E55 (5.9:1 on page); subtle #6F6D64 (4.7:1).

surface#FFFFFF
surface-muted#F1F0EA
sand-light#E1DBD1
line soft#D8D5CE

ink / primary#3A3935
ink-muted#605E55 warm
ink-subtle#6F6D64 warm
