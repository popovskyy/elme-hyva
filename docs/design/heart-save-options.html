FE-78 Heart: save to shopping list

Every frame is live: tap a heart. Gypsophila is already in E2E Showroom, so its heart starts filled. Tapping a filled heart opens the lists, where a ticked row removes it. The header heart is shown on the phone too (today it is desktop-only), otherwise the save has nowhere to land.

1. Burst

Picker on every tap. The heart pops (0.85, 1.2, 1) with a ring and seven dots, then a small heart flies along a curve to the header, which squashes while the badge rolls up.

2. Settle

Picker on every tap. Quieter: the fill grows inside the outline with one soft ring and a tiny lift; no dots, no flight, the header heart beats once and the badge rolls up.

3. Quick save

One tap saves to the last used list with the Burst motion and a toast "Saved to E2E Showroom - Change"; the picker opens only on Change, on a filled heart, or when there are no lists. Works with 1 or 2.
