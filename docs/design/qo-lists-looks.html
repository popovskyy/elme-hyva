FE-76: My Shopping List, list editor and cart

Three directions for the same screens. The list editor frames also stand for the cart page: same rows and bar, the button says Proceed to checkout and the SKU field is not there. Steppers, row totals and the saving state work: press plus or minus. Trash on a list plays the leave animation.

No SKU search endpoint exists yet (BE-11), so the SKU field shows a hint instead of autocomplete in every variant.

1. Order sheet

Closest to today: white cards for lists on the phone, a plain ruled table on desktop, the editor keeps its rows and gets a white sticky bar with the total and the main button.

2. Tiles

Lists become photo tiles (three thumbs as a strip, the whole tile opens the list); editor rows are cards, and the phone bar is dark and opens up to show subtotal and GST.

3. Compact sheet

Dense for repeat ordering: one line per list with icon actions, the SKU field pinned at the top while you scroll, smaller rows and a slim bar with lines and total ex GST.
