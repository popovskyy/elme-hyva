Login page: three looks

Today the login is two plain boxes. Three ways to make it feel like Elme, with the same form (email, password, forgot password, Sign In) and the trade-only message for new retailers. Tick one and press Save, or reply with a number.

Header and footer are stand-ins. Copy follows the B2B rules: trade-only, ABN, prices ex GST; "Create an Account" becomes "Apply for a trade account".

Your pick

1. Split screen

Half the page is a tall flower photo with a line about the trade site; the other half is the sign-in form, and under it a quiet "New to Elme Living?" with the three trade facts and the apply button. The photo drifts very slowly.
