Checkout 3 - totals and the address modal

Option 3 from checkout-options.html, reworked: the order summary is a white card like the steps, the totals get real hierarchy, and "Change" opens the address book in a modal instead of expanding the step. Same labels and numbers as elme-hyva.local/checkout/. Answer with a letter for the totals (A, B or C) and say if the modal is right.

The page

Steps and summary are both white cards on the page colour, with the same hairline. On the phone the summary stays collapsed to one line with the total (as it works today) and the place-order bar sits at the bottom. Totals here are A; the three treatments are compared below.

Totals

Same card, three ways to land the number. Lines above the total stay quiet (muted label, dark figure, no rules between them) so the total is the only loud thing in the card.

A. Receipt

One dark rule, then "Total" with "incl GST" under it and the figure in the serif at 32px. Calmest, closest to a printed invoice.

B. Dark band

The total sits in a charcoal band across the bottom of the card. Loudest; it ties to the dark Place order button.

C. Total with the button

The total and Place order live together at the foot of the summary, so on desktop the decision and the action are in one place.

"Change" - pick an address

Opens over the checkout: a centred dialog on desktop, a sheet from the bottom on the phone. Saved addresses are cards (name, company, street, suburb line, as in the address book); picking one saves it and "Done" closes. "Add a new address" is the last card. "Change" next to "Invoice to:" opens the same dialog titled "Billing address".
