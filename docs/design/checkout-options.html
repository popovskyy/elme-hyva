Checkout - three looks

Same four steps, same order summary, same numbers as elme-hyva.local/checkout/ right now (Address, Delivery, Payment, Delivery instructions; $900.00 subtotal, $45.00 shipping, $94.50 GST, $1,039.50 total). What changes is only how it looks: the step markers, the card chrome, the product placeholder, and how the total is set off. Pick a number, or a mix such as "2 on the phone, 1 on desktop".

Product thumbnail is a placeholder in every option on purpose - today it shows a broken Magento mark on the live checkout, so all three replace it with a plain line icon on a sand tile instead of inventing a real product photo.

1. Quiet ledger

No boxes at all. Steps are separated by a single hairline, step markers are small outline circles that turn sage with a check once done, options are a plain radio row. The summary sits flush in its column with a dashed rule between lines, like a receipt, and the total gets one bold rule above it instead of a block of colour.

2. Connected path

A thin line runs down the left edge and threads through all four step markers, so the page reads as one path instead of four separate forms - done steps are filled sage circles with a check, the open step gets a dark ring. The order summary becomes a warm sand panel, like a packing slip, with the total underlined in the brand's dark ink.

3. Editorial, refined

Keeps the card-per-step shape closest to what is live today, so it is the lowest-risk change: lighter hairline borders instead of heavy ones, step markers as small sand squares that turn sage when done, and the grand total breaks out of the card as a full-width dark band so the number you actually pay is the one thing that pops.
